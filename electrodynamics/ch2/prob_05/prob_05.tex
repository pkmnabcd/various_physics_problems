\begin{flushleft}


In this problem, we find the electric field a distance $z$ above the center of a ring of charge with charge density $\lambda$ and radius $R$.
For this problem, define the coordinate system such that the center of the ring is at the origin, the ring exists in the $xy$-plane, and the $\hat{z}$ direction points from the origin to the point of interest.
Since we are dealing with a line of charge, will use the following equation to find our electric field.
$$\vec{E}(\vec{r})=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int\frac{\lambda(\vec{r'})}{\scripty{r}^2}\hat{\scripty{r}}\,d\ell'$$


\end{flushleft}
